Frisbee

A little disc, a world of possibilities.

About

Frisbee is an interactive web experience built around a 3D frisbee game.

Users can throw a frisbee, aim at targets, and score points.
The project also explores frisbee as a sport, the spirit of fair play, and the joy of playing together.

Pages

* Home: An introduction to frisbee and the experience.
* Playground: The interactive 3D frisbee game.
* Discover: An introduction to frisbee, including basic rules and ways to play.
* Spirit: The spirit of the game, fair play, and frisbee culture.
* Join: Information about frisbee activities and opportunities to play together.
